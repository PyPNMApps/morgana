# Evolve the foundation as the canonical knowledge source

## Promote a lesson

Capture the concrete failure/question, evidence, resolution, applicability and
limits using [lesson.md](../../../templates/project/lesson.md). Separate the reusable
principle from a project's hard-coded path, numeric budget or one-time permission.
Check whether an existing the foundation skill owns it before creating a new skill.
When a lesson is found during consumer work, reconcile the reusable skill change
with its canonical owner before handoff if that checkout is accessible and edits
there are authorized. Do not infer cross-repository write permission from the
consumer task. Otherwise prepare a local learning-update proposal. Keep consumer requirements, screenshots and
concrete implementation evidence local; add no project lineage map. If access or
scope prevents the the foundation update, identify the pending owner/update instead of
claiming synchronization. Review adoption separately; never rewrite other
consumers automatically.

Review conflicting lessons and state the chosen rule or decision branches locally.
Write the examples and contracts needed to apply the method as generic the foundation
guidance. Do not add repository lineage maps. Actual copied/adapted material
retains its license and required notices under third-party-reuse.

## Change review

Update the canonical skill and relevant local procedure/template/tool together.
Record what changed, why, compatibility/adoption impact and validation in the
[learning update template](../../../templates/project/learning-update.md).
Prefer supported corrections over accumulating exceptions with no clear scope.
Mark proposals/experimental advice separately from validated methods. Record the applicable
review date and evidence level; never turn an old result into current proof.

## Adoption and retirement

Consumers record the the foundation revision and local overrides. Updates are explicit diffs,
not automatic overwrite. If a rule becomes obsolete, explain its replacement and
refresh related references/indexes/facts. Keep historical validation records labeled by their actual scope.
Run portability and skill/link validation before handoff. A fresh the foundation clone should
contain the needed guidance, references and templates without another checkout.
