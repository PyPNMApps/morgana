# Third-party reuse and notices

Apply before importing or adapting third-party material and when changing its
packaging or distribution. This is a portable workflow for future repositories,
not a claim that the foundation ships any particular third-party component.

Read the [procedure](references/procedure.md) and adopt the
[third-party notices template](../../templates/project/third-party-notices.md).
Keep original file notices, required license text and applicable upstream NOTICE
entries. Record local modifications without relabeling upstream authorship. A
notices inventory complements license obligations; it does not replace them.

Follow [coding-style](../coding-style/SKILL.md) for language-valid file banners and
[knowledge-freshness](../knowledge-freshness/SKILL.md) for current official contracts.
Missing or incompatible permission is a reuse decision gap, not permission to copy.
