# Third-party notices

placeholders and repeat the component section for actual reused material only.
Do not fabricate entries or treat this inventory as permission to reuse.

### Required upstream notice text

Insert the applicable text without silently editing its meaning. Keep licensing
terms in their required full-text files; a URL or this summary is not a substitute.

### Local modification notice

Describe the actual changes; retain upstream copyright separately. Do not claim
ownership of unchanged third-party work.
